# 🎬 SJ Digital Creatures AI Incubator — Demo Video Script

> **URL**: http://localhost:8080  
> **Duration**: ~3–5 minutes  
> **Style**: Screen recording with voiceover narration

---

## 🎙️ Voiceover Intro (0:00 – 0:15)

> *"This is the SJ Digital Creatures AI Company Incubator — a fully local, zero-cloud command center for managing an entire portfolio of software projects through 7 specialized AI agents."*

---

## Scene 1 — Main Floor (0:15 – 0:45)

**Action**: Open http://localhost:8080, let page load fully.  
**Show**: The dark glassmorphism main floor with all 8 department room cards.

**Voiceover**:
> *"The Main Floor acts as your company headquarters. Every department has its own dedicated room — Engineering, R&D, Operations, CEO, Sales, Marketing, Finance — and the Sono Room: the Chief Technical Owner's command center."*

**Highlight**: Hover over 2–3 room cards to show glow effects.

---

## Scene 2 — Sono Room / CTO Command Center (0:45 – 1:30)

**Action**: Click the 👑 Sono Room card.  
**Show**: Portfolio dashboard with all git repos detected.

**Voiceover**:
> *"The Sono Room is the executive cockpit. It automatically scans every project in your portfolio — detecting tech stacks, estimating progress, reading git commit history, and identifying the development stage."*

**Show**:
- Project cards with progress bars (Alpha / Beta / Release Candidate / Live)
- Stack badges (Tauri/Rust, Next.js, Python, etc.)
- Last commit timestamps

**Voiceover**:
> *"One click sets any project as Active — instantly pointing all 7 AI agents at that codebase for real-time analysis."*

---

## Scene 3 — CTO Directives Dispatcher (1:30 – 2:15)

**Action**: Scroll to the Directives section in Sono Room.  
**Show**: The directive form with target selector, category dropdown, and preset buttons.

**Voiceover**:
> *"Sono can dispatch authoritative technical directives to the entire AI team. Choose a department, pick a category — Architecture, Performance, Security, Deadlines — then fire a directive."*

**Action**: Click a preset like *"Enforce 100% offline local inference"*.  
**Show**: Agent response cards appearing for MAX, SAGE, OTTO, ARIA, ALEX, NOVA, PENNY.

**Voiceover**:
> *"Every agent responds instantly — powered by local Ollama LLM when available, or the built-in AI engine as fallback. All responses are timestamped and persisted."*

---

## Scene 4 — Engineering Room / MAX (2:15 – 2:50)

**Action**: Go back to Main Floor → click Engineering room.  
**Show**: Live code scanner results, AST analysis, gap detection.

**Voiceover**:
> *"MAX, the Chief Technical Lead, gives you a live architecture analysis of the active project — flagging missing test harnesses, uncommitted changes, containerization gaps, and documentation deficits."*

**Show**: CTO Approvals list — actionable advice cards with risk levels.

---

## Scene 5 — War Room / Morning Meeting (2:50 – 3:30)

**Action**: Navigate to War Room or Meeting Room.  
**Show**: Daily alignment meeting transcript with all 7 agents speaking.

**Voiceover**:
> *"The War Room runs a dynamic daily alignment meeting — every morning and evening. All 7 agents speak about the active project's status, blockers, and deliverables. It's a fully automated standup driven by real project data."*

---

## Scene 6 — Finance Room / Revenue Tracker (3:30 – 4:00)

**Action**: Navigate to Finance room.  
**Show**: Revenue state, project monetization toggles, margin projections.

**Voiceover**:
> *"PENNY, the CFO agent, tracks revenue state across all portfolio projects — marking which are live, computing gross margin, and projecting unit economics for local vs cloud compute strategies."*

---

## Scene 7 — Outro (4:00 – 4:30)

**Action**: Return to Main Floor, slowly pan across all rooms.

**Voiceover**:
> *"SJ Digital Creatures AI Incubator — a fully offline, privacy-first company operating system. 7 AI agents. Zero cloud dependency. 100% local intelligence."*

**Show**: Fade on the SJ Digital Creatures branding.

## 📋 Recording Checklist

- [ ] OBS or built-in screen recorder open and capturing
- [ ] Browser at http://localhost:8080 (full screen or windowed at 1920×1080)
- [ ] Dev server running (`python server.py` in terminal)
- [ ] Microphone ready for voiceover (or record silent + add voiceover in post)
- [ ] Disable notifications during recording

## 🎨 Post-Production Tips

- Add **title cards** between scenes with section names
- Use **zoom-in** on key elements (progress bars, agent response cards)
- Add **ambient music** (lo-fi / cinematic tech vibes)
- Export at **1080p60** for best quality
